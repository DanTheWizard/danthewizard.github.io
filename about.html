About Me

My name is Daniel Palmik, also known as Dan The Wizard. I am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Electrical and Computer Engineering Technology at the New York Institute of Technology.   • System Administration: working with users, groups, policies, servers, and services.

Experience and Learning

As an IT Support Technician Intern with the New York City Department of Education, I work with hardware and software issues, maintain classroom technology, and support staff with daily technical needs in a high school environment.

Outside of that role, I do freelance website and technical support work. This includes website maintenance, domain setup, DNS configuration, Cloudflare security, hosting setup, and troubleshooting technical problems for clients.

I also completed the Google Cybersecurity Certificate, which helped strengthen my understanding of networking, Linux, security operations, risk management, and cybersecurity fundamentals.

How I Approach Technology

I try to approach technology with a security-first mindset. That means I care about making systems efficient, but also reducing unnecessary exposure, planning for recovery, and making sure the system can be maintained later.

When I do not know something, I research it, test it, break it down, and try to understand why it works. That mindset is what pushes me to keep learning and building.
